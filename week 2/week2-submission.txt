Energy consumption in Robotics: A simplified modeling approach. 
(2024, 05 Nov.). https://arxiv.org/html/2411.03194v1 

Reflection on the pertinence of the reference: 

This reference will contribute to the project, because our goal of the project is 
to visualize a robotic arm where the mechanical energy consumption is optimized as much 
as possible. In order to do this, we would use reinforcement learning and use set 
parameters to reward the AI. The power consumption would be one of the parameters that the raw 
data would be built on and then presented. Therefore, understanding the formula of torque 
to find the mechanical power of a robotic using the sum of its joints is essential 
to get viable results and curate the data as much as possible to not have 5 million rows 
of data in our CSV. We have to filter the trials of the AI by those set parameters
during the learning process to be able to optimize the movement trials and also to be able 
to do vector integration in our Unity simulation to show how the data was built. For 
example, we would have to show the velocity and the torque vectors visually to understand
the different values that we got for the training on the website. Using the concepts
of inertial dynamics and the other concepts and formulas of the reference would allow 
the logic of the implementation of the joints movements to work. These formulas would have
to be integrated using C#.
